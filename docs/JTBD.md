# Jobs To Be Done

## Primary functional JTBD

When I have a rough software idea but cannot express it like a professional product and engineering team, help me clarify exactly what I mean, challenge the important gaps in language I understand, let me approve and lock it, and translate that locked intent into instructions that an AI coding agent can execute without drifting away from my product.

## Emotional JTBD

Help me remain the product owner even when the AI builder is more technically capable than I am.

I should understand what is being built, why important decisions were made, what changed, and what I still need to decide.

## Continuity JTBD

When my coding agent runs out of tokens/context, fails, or I want to switch tools, let another coding agent continue without making me reconstruct the whole project from memory.

## Professionalization JTBD

When my vibe-coded project becomes serious enough for a real product/engineering team, provide the professional product and technical context they need without forcing them to reverse-engineer AI chat history.

## Verification JTBD

When an AI builder says something is complete, show me what is actually proven, what is merely implemented, what remains uncertain, and exactly what I should personally test.

## Portability JTBD

Let me move the same product intent between Codex, Claude Code, Cursor, Lovable and future AI builders without rewriting the product definition for each platform.
