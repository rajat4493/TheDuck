## AI system / AIOps note

- AI jobs: Help Independent podcasters who publish weekly and edit their own shows with: Turn each finished episode into show notes, chapters and promo posts without spending two hours writing them
- Failure behaviour: A person reviews and can edit every AI result before it is used or shared.
- Data boundary: Personal information may be sent to an outside AI provider. Confirm the provider's data-use and retention terms before real use.
- Provider portability: Keep the AI provider behind one interface so it can be swapped without changing product behaviour.
- Evaluation: Build a small evaluation set of realistic, made-up inputs covering normal, edge and failure cases before trusting outputs. Fluent output is not evidence of correctness.
- Quality signals: How often users edit or reject AI output; Errors caught by review; Time saved versus doing it by hand
- Observability: Run ID per AI call; Model and version; Latency; Tokens and estimated cost; Errors and fallbacks; Redaction of personal information in logs
