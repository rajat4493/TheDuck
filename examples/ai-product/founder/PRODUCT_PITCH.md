# How to explain ShowNotes AI

## In one line

ShowNotes AI is for Independent podcasters who publish weekly and edit their own shows.

## The problem

Turn each finished episode into show notes, chapters and promo posts without spending two hours writing them

## What is better with it

Publishing an episode's written material takes under 15 minutes instead of about two hours

## How it works

1. Upload an episode audio file
2. Get a draft of show notes, chapter markers and three social posts
3. Review and edit the AI's result before it is used or shared
4. Copy the final text into their podcast host and social accounts

## What it deliberately does not do (yet)

- Posting directly to social media accounts
- Audio editing
- Hosting or publishing the podcast
