# What you are building

You are building **ShowNotes AI** for Independent podcasters who publish weekly and edit their own shows.

## The job it does for them

Turn each finished episode into show notes, chapters and promo posts without spending two hours writing them

## What will be better when it works

Publishing an episode's written material takes under 15 minutes instead of about two hours

## The main journey

1. Upload an episode audio file
2. Get a draft of show notes, chapter markers and three social posts
3. Review and edit the AI's result before it is used or shared
4. Copy the final text into their podcast host and social accounts

## What the first version will not do

- Posting directly to social media accounts
- Audio editing
- Hosting or publishing the podcast

## Things it must never do

- Must not use uploaded audio to train AI models.
- Must not publish, send or act on AI output without the user's approval.

## How you will know it is done

- Five podcasters use it on a real episode and say they would use it again
- A success check awaiting your decision: The AI cost of one episode stays under the agreed cost limit (it cannot be used to decide "done" until you decide — see "Still waiting for you")

## What kind of product this is

- This is a pilot with a small number of real users.
- It holds basic personal details (such as names, emails or accounts).
- It uses AI to do part of the job.
- It connects to: A speech-to-text and AI writing service — to transcribe the episode and draft the text.
