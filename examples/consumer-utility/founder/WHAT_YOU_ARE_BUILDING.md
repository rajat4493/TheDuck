# What you are building

You are building **FreshNudge** for Busy home cooks who do one big weekly shop.

## The job it does for them

Keep track of what is in the fridge and use food before it expires, without writing lists

## What will be better when it works

They throw away less food because they get a nudge the day before something expires

## The main journey

1. Add an item and its expiry date in under 10 seconds
2. See everything sorted by what expires first
3. Get a reminder the day before an item expires
4. Mark an item as used or thrown away

## What the first version will not do

- Recipe suggestions
- Barcode or receipt scanning
- Sharing a fridge with other people

## Things it must never do

- Send more than one reminder per day

## How you will know it is done

- I use it for two weeks and throw away less food than before

## What kind of product this is

- This is a demo to show people (not for real use yet).
- It holds no personal information.
- It does not use AI.
- It does not connect to other services.
