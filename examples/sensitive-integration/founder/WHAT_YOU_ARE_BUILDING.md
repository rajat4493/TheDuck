# What you are building

You are building **PhysioPath** for Patients of one partner physiotherapy clinic who have a home exercise plan.

## The job it does for them

Know exactly which exercises to do at home and how often, and see changes their physio makes without waiting for the next appointment

## What will be better when it works

Patients follow their plan correctly between appointments and stop relying on paper handouts

## The main journey

1. Patient signs in with an invite from the clinic
2. Patient sees today's exercises with instructions
3. Patient marks exercises as done
4. Physio changes the plan in the clinic's system and the patient sees the update

## What the first version will not do

- Diagnosis, medical advice or symptom checking
- Booking appointments or taking payments
- Messaging between patients and physios
- Any clinic other than the one partner clinic

## Things it must never do

- Must not show one patient's information to another patient.
- Must not change anything in the clinic's practice management system.
- Must not use real people's sensitive information before a qualified security and privacy review is done (use made-up test data until then).

## How you will know it is done

- Ten pilot patients use it for four weeks
- The partner clinic confirms the plans shown match their system

## What kind of product this is

- This is a real product that people will rely on.
- It holds sensitive or regulated information.
- TheDuck assumes it does not use AI (not confirmed).
- It connects to: The clinic's practice management system — the source of each patient's exercise plan.
