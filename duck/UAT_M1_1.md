# M1.1 owner review

Run `PORT=4318 npm start` and open `http://127.0.0.1:4318`.

Try these checks:

1. Enter a rough idea like “I want to take a photo of an antique and tell me if it is valuable and why.” Does “Here’s what I heard” feel like a useful reflection? Is the value question genuinely material?
2. Try a simple idea such as a private reading journal. Does it reach a shaped preview without making you fill out a product-management form?
3. Accept one suggestion and reject another. Confirm accepted text is marked as a TheDuck suggestion and rejected text does not appear in the lock or packs.
4. Use “You misunderstood me,” then “Almost — change something.” Confirm your correction retains earlier choices and changes the shaped product.
5. In Idea Fidelity, read every “You said” row. Mark only statements that are genuinely captured. Confirm unresolved statements keep the lock disabled.
6. Lock the shaped idea. Open all three packs and check that product identity, audience, outcome, scope, and completion checks agree.
7. Read `VAT_UAT.md` in the Founder pack. It should tell you how to test the idea in ordinary language and state that the underlying product is not yet built.
8. Verify downloads in your normal browser. If downloads are unavailable, expand “Copy your lock and packs as text” and save the JSON manually.

Record whether the shaped idea is clearer than the original, whether any inference was too confident, and which question or correction felt unnecessary. This review is required before M1.1 can be marked complete. Do not begin M2 from this checkpoint.
