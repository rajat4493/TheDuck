# Owner review — M1

1. Run `PORT=4317 npm start` and open http://127.0.0.1:4317 in your browser.
2. Describe a real rough idea. Check whether the questions help you resolve something important instead of making you rewrite the idea as a long specification.
3. Enter your intended user, outcome, journey and completion checks. Include an uncertain or difficult requirement.
4. In review, decline one suggestion or accept one that you actually want. Read the final preview: does it describe your product, with suggestions clearly attributed?
5. Confirm the lock action is unavailable before you explicitly approve. Approve only when the preview is right.
6. Open each of the three packs. Check that the user, purpose, scope and completion checks stay the same. Are the founder instructions understandable? Could a builder and product team use the other packs?
7. Download the canonical lock and all pack files. Confirm files really appear and contain your words. If downloads are unavailable, expand “Copy your lock and packs as text”, select and copy the JSON into a text file.
8. Reload. Confirm the same lock returns. Save your exports before starting another idea: storage keeps the latest lock only.

For each issue, record what you entered, what appeared, what you expected, and why it matters. The next owner decision is whether clarification quality meets M1 or needs more work. Do not approve M2 merely because files were generated.
