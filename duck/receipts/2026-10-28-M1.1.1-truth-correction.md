## What was requested and why (HUMAN)

The product owner reviewed the M1.1 branch and generated examples and found four
defects in what the packs told people. They asked for a narrow correction, with no
new capability: (1) reduce the question burden, (2) never hide an unresolved
material decision, (3) separate locked commitments from draft acceptance criteria,
(4) render every must-not item as an explicit prohibition. No UI, Next.js,
Playwright, live AI or other new capability.

## Why the M1.1 tests did not catch this

The M1.1 tests passed and still prove what they tested: the lock, fingerprints,
provenance, Change Proposals, drift guards and cross-pack agreement. But they
checked that packs **agreed with the lock**, not whether a pack was **materially
misleading** to the person reading it. A pack can repeat the lock faithfully and
still say "nothing is waiting for your decision" while the lock itself depends on
a limit nobody has set. The curated founder-language assertions checked that
required sentences were present, not that they were true.

Observed in the committed M1.1 examples (`git show eaa5bb6:examples/...`):

| Defect | Observed in M1.1 output |
|---|---|
| Question burden | 13 / 14 / 12 questions for the three samples (`examples/README.md`); up to 16 possible |
| Hidden decision | `ai-product/founder/WHAT_YOU_ARE_BUILDING.md` line 37 has "…under the agreed cost limit"; line 48 says "Nothing is waiting for your decision right now." |
| Journey steps as acceptance criteria | `ai-product/builder/ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md` has 4 "Main journey step N works…" criteria labelled as locked acceptance criteria |
| Bare forbidden actions | Under "Things it must never do": "- Use uploaded audio to train AI models" |

## What changed, and the credibility risk each change removes

| # | Change | Risk removed | Provenance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The rules provider now asks **5 core questions** (who + job, outcome, journey, boundaries, success) and **at most 3 follow-ups**, each only when an answer changes the product: a too-broad user, AI mentioned, sensitive data mentioned. Every question offers "I am not sure yet", which creates a visible open question. The gate refuses a provider that goes over either limit. Name, stage, connected services and similar detail move to the editable preview; when they are not given, they appear as an explicit assumption or an open decision. | A founder put off by a long form before seeing any value; guessed answers recorded as decisions. | HUMAN |
| 2 | New unresolved-reference check (`assurance/open-items.ts`). Statements that rely on an undefined limit, threshold, value, policy or provider (e.g. "agreed cost limit", "TBD", "appropriate retention", "to be decided", "a … service") become founder decision cards, or professional open questions for a generic provider. They are shown in WHAT_YOU_ARE_BUILDING, CURRENT_OUTCOME, the Professional Pack, the Builder Pack and the Universal handover. The founder renderer refuses to output "Nothing is waiting for your decision" while anything is open. Assumptions are listed separately from decisions. | A founder told nothing is pending while "done" depends on a number they never set; builders silently picking that number. | HUMAN |
| 3 | Only must-not rules, measurable success checks and owner-written criteria become **LOCKED ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA**, in "When …, the system shall …" form. Journey steps, and success checks that cannot be measured yet, become **DRAFT VERIFICATION CANDIDATES** (VC-…). Each lists what is missing and is marked OPEN QUESTION. The Builder, Professional and Universal packs tell the next agent to refine them with the product owner before claiming anything is complete. | Professionals and agents treating "Upload an episode audio file works" as implementation-ready, testable acceptance. | HUMAN |
| 4 | Every must-not item renders as "Must not ….", e.g. "Must not use uploaded audio to train AI models." The stored founder wording is unchanged. | A forbidden action under a "must never" heading being read as an instruction to do it. | HUMAN |

Implementation consequences (IMPLEMENTATION-NECESSARY):

- **Locked intent schema:**
  - stage, data sensitivity and "uses AI" may now be locked as undecided;
  - out-of-scope may be empty if there is at least one must-not rule;
  - acceptance criteria may be empty;
  - acceptance-criterion `source` no longer allows `workflow`.
- Questions carry a `tier` (core / follow-up). Every question must have a not-sure option. There is a new combined "parts" question type.
- The provider id changed from `theduck-rules-v1` to `theduck-rules-v2`. Nothing was persisted under v1 (fixtures only), so no data migration was needed.

Found while reading the regenerated output (EVIDENCE-DISCOVERED, fixed before commit):

- Consumer utility still said "Nothing is waiting for your decision" while its journey checks still needed the founder's input. Draft candidates now count as open items.
- Draft-check grammar ("When Independent podcasters … does") was rewritten as "When the user carries out …".
- AI and data assumptions were listed twice in "Assumed" sections. Now de-duplicated.
